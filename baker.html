Known behaviours

Baker is the matriarch, the first arrival, and the reason there is a court at all. She rules from elevation and rarely explains herself.

  • B1.1Selects her own humans. Has done this once. Considers the matter closed and the ruling final.
  • B1.2Emits a war cry before play. The household investigated the first time. The household was fine.
  • B1.3Guards the carrier. It is her carrier. There is a name written on it.
  • B1.4Once said hello to strangers in the next lane on the freeway. No explanation was offered.
  • B1.5Has a heart-shaped nose. Refuses to acknowledge this in any way.
