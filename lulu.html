L3 · Garden Kitty

Lulu

The dramatic one. The ultimate sweetheart.

Lulu appeared out of the garden like a mystery no one had noticed yet, and she has been emoting theatrically about it ever since. She holds hands, she sits like a person, and she sleeps like the world is at peace.

The file on Lulu

Everything below is true. Mostly. The full story is saved for the books.

  • For weeks, something was nibbling the garden. The family blamed a raccoon. The raccoon was innocent.
  • Won over instantly by a borrowed pom pom. Its owner has been gracious about it.
  • Has learned to operate the air conditioner. Settings and all. We don’t know either.
  • Eats cucumbers. On purpose. Enjoys them.

Sitting like a person

She was home before anyone saw her.
